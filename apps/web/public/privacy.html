Privacy Policy

Effective October 8, 2026

GTOtutor (gtotutor.app) is a poker training app run by Jordan Cao. This policy explains what we collect, why, who it's shared with, and how to delete it. Questions: jordancao77@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account details. If you sign in with Google, we receive your email address and the profile details Google shares (name and profile picture). If you sign up with email, we store your email address and a hashed password; we never see or store the password itself.
  • Your practice data. The hands you play, your decisions and how they were graded, stars, notes and tags you add, session reviews, and your conversations with the AI coach.
  • Guest play. If you play without an account, your browser gets a random guest ID, and your hands are linked to it. A guest ID doesn't identify you.
  • Technical data. Like any website, our hosting providers process your IP address and browser details to deliver pages and protect against abuse. These may appear in server logs, which we keep for up to 30 days.

Browser storage

We use your browser's local storage, not tracking cookies, for things the app needs: your sign-in session, your guest ID and practice session, your settings, and whether you've seen the tour. We don't use analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and we don't sell your data.

How we use it

  • To run the trainer: deal hands, grade decisions and show your history.
  • To save your progress to your account and carry guest hands over when you sign up.
  • To answer your questions with the AI coach.
  • To keep the service secure and working, including usage limits that prevent abuse.

Who we share it with

We share data only with the services that run GTOtutor, and only what each one needs:

  • Supabase handles sign-in and stores our database.
  • Amazon Web Services hosts the website and the app's servers in the United States.
  • Anthropic powers the AI coach. When you open a coach explanation or send it a message, the details of that hand and your message are sent to Anthropic's API to generate the reply. We don't send your name or email. Under its commercial terms, Anthropic doesn't use this data to train its models.
  • Google, only if you choose to sign in with Google.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

  • Guest data is deleted when you start a new guest session or after 24 hours without a new hand, unless you sign up during the session.
  • Account data is kept until you delete your account. You can do that anytime from the account menu in the app; it deletes your account and all your practice data. Deleted data may remain in backups for a short period before it's overwritten.

Your choices and rights

You can play without an account, delete your account from the app, or email us to access, correct, export or delete your data. We'll respond within 30 days. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local privacy law; contact us to exercise them.

Children

GTOtutor isn't for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, email us and we'll delete it.

Security

All traffic uses HTTPS, only the app's server can read the database, and access to our systems is limited. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above. For significant changes, we'll give notice in the app.
