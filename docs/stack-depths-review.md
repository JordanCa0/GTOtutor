## Findings (2026-10-12)

- **Ranges look sane.** First-in ranges barely move between 20, 60 and 100bb (UTG about 19% at every depth); at 20bb the BTN tightens (36% vs 41%) and the SB widens (77%) with a quarter of its range shoving. No premium hand ever folds first in, UTG never plays trash, and little of any range is mixed (at most 10% per profile).
- **Facing a 20bb shove, the BB calls tighter from earlier positions:** 5% vs UTG, 10% vs BTN, 17% vs SB. That's the expected ordering.
- **Short seats at deeper tables mostly play like the equal-stacks chart of their depth** (median 7.6% of the range differs).
- **One systematic exception, for you to decide: a 20bb SB at a 100bb or 60bb table.** It differs by about 42% from the 20bb SB. That isn't a solver fault: it follows from the plan's rule that each player's sizes come from their own stack. Against a 20bb SB, a 100bb BB plays the deep tree:
  - it can isolate a limp to 3.5 (an equal-stacks 20bb BB can only shove);
  - it can 3-bet to 10 instead of shoving;
  - so the 20bb SB almost stops limping (0.9% vs 25.5%) and raises to 2 instead.

  The other way to model it is for a deep player facing only short stacks to use the short tree. That changes `tier_node` in `preflop/src/game.rs` and the engine, and needs every mixed profile re-solved (about 20 minutes). Nothing ships wrong either way: the charts and the engine use the same tree.
